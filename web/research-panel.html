Detection accepts either a clear denoised amplitude increase OR 120 ms burst energy combined with waveform change above seated-rest noise. Both paths use a 30 ms evidence check. No 0.5-second hold is needed. Activation does not verify standing posture. Raw ADC is always saved.

Changing either setting starts a new five-second resting calibration. Settings are locked during recording and games. Calibrate while seated and still, then check that rest stays QUIET and a new contraction produces ACTIVATION DETECTED.

Session controls

Connect and check the live signal first. Then close this panel, complete the four checks, and select Start Session. Lock a bus with gaze or mouse, then make a new short activation. Confirmation advances the task automatically. No observer buttons or 0.5-second hold are required. One activation counts once; let the signal settle before the next selection.

Standard: 3 sets × 10 repetitions. Fast demo: 1 set × 2 repetitions. Completed counts are confirmed task selections, not verified complete stands.
